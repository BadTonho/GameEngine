# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want GameEngine to be a welcoming, respectful and technically constructive project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or role.

## Expected behavior

- communicate respectfully and assume good intentions;
- focus criticism on ideas, code and behavior rather than people;
- explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning;
- welcome questions from new contributors;
- respect project boundaries, licenses and the privacy of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks;
- publishing private or sensitive information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting project infrastructure or collaboration;
- abusive, sexualized or hateful language;
- sustained bad-faith behavior that prevents constructive collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions will consider the context, severity and pattern of the behavior.

## Reporting

Report serious or private concerns directly to the maintainers through a private GitHub contact method. Do not include private details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
